What is a Wardley map?

A Wardley map is a picture of a business strategy. Its vertical axis is a value chain: what your users need at the top, and the components that meet those needs below. Its horizontal axis is evolution: how mature each component is, from Genesis through Custom-built and Product to Commodity.

Seeing where each part of your business sits, and where it is moving, shows what to build, what to buy and what to outsource. The method was created by Simon Wardley; Learn Wardley Mapping is a good introduction.

Why map as a team?

The value of a map is the conversation it starts. In MapTogether the whole team edits one map in real time, in a workshop or remotely, instead of passing a picture around.
